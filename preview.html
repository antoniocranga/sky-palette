Sky Palette v1.2.0

Physical sky color engine & solar architecture

Sky Palette calculates natural 5-swatch color palettes (Zenith, Mid-Sky, Horizon, Sun Glow, Cloud Tint) from physically-based solar scattering models, real-time meteorological cloud dynamics, and moderated crowdsourced sky photography.

GitHub Repository Report a Bug Release Notes

Core Technologies

Preetham & Hosek-Wilkie
Physical atmospheric scattering equations modeling Rayleigh (air) and Mie (aerosol) sunlight absorption.
SunCalc Solar Physics
Sub-degree astronomical solar elevation, azimuth, and golden/twilight phase calculation by latitude and longitude.
Open-Meteo Solar API
Real-time stratified cloud layering (low, mid, high) and diffuse radiation without API keys.
Vanilla Modern Web
Fast zero-runtime architecture with HTML5 Canvas, CSS Grid, and Tailwind CSS. Zero bloat.
Cloudflare D1 & R2 Storage
Serverless SQLite at the edge and zero-egress photo object storage for community contributions.
Cloudflare Edge & Analytics
Global edge caching, TLS security, and non-invasive privacy-preserving web telemetry.

Usage Monitoring & Telemetry

Cloudflare Web Analytics (Privacy-First)

To monitor application health, traffic volume, and performance (such as Largest Contentful Paint and interaction responsiveness), Sky Palette utilizes Cloudflare Web Analytics.

  • Zero Cookies: No cookies or client-side storage are ever set or read for telemetry.
  • Zero Fingerprinting: No device fingerprints, canvas hashes, or cross-site tracking IDs.
  • Zero Personal Data: IP addresses are never logged or stored. Aggregate metrics only.
  • GDPR & ePrivacy Compliant: Does not require consent banners because no personal data is processed.

Privacy & Cookies

100% Cookie-Free

Sky Palette is built on strict data minimalism:

No User Accounts: Anyone can explore palettes and submit photos anonymously.
Zero Cookies: No database or session cookies. Cloudflare D1 and Workers operate via edge bindings without tracking.

Sky Palette Privacy Policy (Summary)

1. Information We Do Not Collect: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or payment data. We do not use cookies, local storage tracking keys, or third-party advertising pixels.

2. Crowdsourced Sky Photos: When you submit a sky image, EXIF metadata (timestamp and approximate location) is extracted locally in your browser to evaluate solar physics alignment. Photos are stored anonymously and released under CC0 (Public Domain).

3. Telemetry & Infrastructure: Usage is monitored exclusively through privacy-preserving aggregate telemetry via Cloudflare Web Analytics without tracking individual users or collecting IP addresses.

Sky Palette Terms of Service (Summary)

1. Open Source & Free Use: Sky Palette is free and open-source software provided under the MIT License. You are free to use generated color palettes for personal, commercial, and artistic purposes.

2. Contribution Terms: By sharing a photo through Sky Palette, you confirm you are the copyright holder and agree to irrevocably dedicate your contribution to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.
