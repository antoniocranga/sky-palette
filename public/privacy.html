Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Sky Palette is a small, anonymous tool. This page lists every piece of data it can store, without legal padding.

What is stored when you browse

  • No cookies and no tracking identifiers.
  • One browser preference: the city slug you last chose, in localStorage under sky-palette.city. It contains only a slug such as tokyo-japan. Clearing site data removes it.
  • Server logs record the request path, status, duration and a random request id. Query strings are redacted; raw IP addresses are not logged.

What is stored when you submit a photo

  • The photo itself, with camera metadata (EXIF, GPS, device) stripped on the server before it is written to storage.
  • The location label you choose and the coordinates used for the palette.
  • The capture time, the five computed colours, and your anonymous handle.
  • A salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address, used only to enforce the upload limit (5 per hour). The raw address is never stored.
  • A record that you confirmed the CC0 dedication, with a timestamp.

Where it lives

Photos are stored in Cloudflare R2 and their records in Cloudflare D1, in Cloudflare's European region. The application runs on Cloudflare Workers. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo and is fetched by the server, not your browser.

How long

Approved photos stay published until you ask for removal. Pending or rejected photos are kept for moderation and are removed when rejected or deleted. Rate-limit counters are pruned automatically.

Your control

Write to the project issue tracker with the photo URL or handle and ask for deletion. Deletion removes the image from storage and the database row; the request path then returns 404. No account is required.

Licence

Published photos are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting, storage, Turnstile bot protection) and Open-Meteo (weather). Turnstile is used only on the upload form; it may set its own short-lived challenge state, governed by Cloudflare's privacy terms.
